Loopsprout

Stack layers of instruments, hum a tune to turn it into notes, and hit play. Every note is locked to the key, so nothing sounds wrong.

Layers

Your voice

Hum for one loop after a 4-beat count-in. Then turn it into notes on any instrument.

  • Glowing cells fit the chord. Start there and it always sounds good.
  • Faint dots show notes from your other layers, so you can line parts up.
  • Drag across a row for a long note. Click a note again to erase it.
  • Play live with A–L. Turn on Record keys while playing to write into the selected layer.
