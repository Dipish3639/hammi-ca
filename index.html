Welcome to hammi.ca

Small games for the PC and the browser, a music maker, a shelf for all your games, a chat app and a wall you can paint on.

games07

  • 01

    orbit climb

    browser · 3d

    Orbit Climb

    NewBrowser3DParkourController2 players

    Climb through three worlds before the flood catches you: the Concrete Spire, Water World and Ember Peak. Wall-run, double jump and air dash from ledge to ledge, beat the boss at the top of every layer, and take down the final boss to conquer the world. Every jump, dash and boss has its own sound. Play alone or with a friend in split-screen co-op. Power cells you collect buy permanent upgrades in the Workshop.

    Play now

    Plays in your browser on a computer, with keyboard and mouse or a game controller. Co-op needs a controller for player 2. Press M to turn the sound on or off.

  • 02

    loopsprout

    browser · music

    Loopsprout

    NewBrowserMusic

    Make a little loop of music. Stack layers of instruments and drums, hum a tune into your mic and it turns into notes, then hit play. Every note is locked to the key, so nothing sounds wrong.

    Make music

    Plays in your browser. Humming needs a microphone; you can upload a recording instead. Your loop is saved in your browser.

  • 03

    loose thread

    browser · pixel art

    Loose Thread

    NewBrowserPixel art

    You have a spool of thread and no weapons. Run circles around creatures and cross your own thread to pull the loop tight and tie them up. Light four lanterns in each room to open the door, and get through one long afternoon.

    Play now

    Plays in your browser. Keyboard on a computer; touch controls on a phone.

  • 04

    house edge

    windows · 3d

    House Edge

    Windows3DOnline

    A small 3D casino for Windows. Walk up to a table and play blackjack, dice, slots or roulette, alone or online with other players. It uses play chips only, so no real money is involved, and you sign in with a Nookara account.

    DownloadComing soon
  • 05

    pixel wall

    browser · multiplayer
    wall.hammi.ca128 × 64

    Pixel Wall

    NewBrowserMultiplayer

    One shared canvas that anyone can paint on. Every pixel costs Nookara credits, and you sign in with a Nookara account to buy them. Your pixels stay on the wall for everyone to see.

    Open the wall
  • 06

    gameshelf

    windows · launcher

    GameShelf

    NewWindowsLauncher

    All your games on one shelf, with big box art. Your Steam and Epic games show up by themselves, and when you add a folder of game files GameShelf works out which console each one is for and sets up an emulator to play it. No games are included; bring your own.

    Download for Windows

    Version 0.1.2, 64-bit Windows, 158 MB zip. Nothing to install: unzip it anywhere and run GameShelf.exe.

  • 07

    hammi chat

    browser · chat

    Hammi Chat

    NewBrowserChat

    A chat app for Ember servers, made here on hammi.ca. Sign in with your Nookara account to talk in your servers and send direct messages. Encrypted DMs, signed messages and custom emoji all work, and you can do almost everything from the keyboard.

    Open Hammi ChatTry the demo

    Runs in your browser on a computer or phone. Signing in is limited to testers while the app waits for Nookara to verify it; anyone can try the demo.
