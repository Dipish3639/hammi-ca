NewBrowser3DParkourController2 players

New game: Orbit Climb

Climb through three worlds before the flood catches you: the Concrete Spire, Water World and Ember Peak. Wall-run, double jump and air dash from ledge to ledge, beat the boss at the top of every layer, and take down the final boss to conquer the world. Power cells you collect buy permanent upgrades in the Workshop.

  • 2-player split-screen co-op. Player 2 uses a controller, you share the flood and the bosses, and if one of you goes down, you come back next to your partner after 5 seconds.
  • Sound: effects for jumps, pickups and bosses, and a low hum for each world. Press M to mute.
  • Works with a game controller, menus included. Keyboard and mouse still work too.
  • Three worlds to pick from, a final boss in each, and the Workshop on the main menu.
Play Orbit Climb

Plays in your browser on a computer, with keyboard and mouse or a game controller. Co-op needs a controller for player 2.

NewBrowserMusic

New: Loopsprout

Make a little loop of music. Stack layers of instruments and drums, hum a tune into your mic and it turns into notes, then hit play. Every note is locked to the key, so nothing sounds wrong.

Make music

Plays in your browser. Humming needs a microphone; you can upload a recording instead. Your loop is saved in your browser.

New game: Loose Thread

You have a spool of thread and no weapons. Run circles around creatures and cross your own thread to pull the loop tight and tie them up. Light four lanterns in each room to open the door, and get through one long afternoon.

Play Loose Thread

Plays in your browser. Keyboard on a computer; touch controls on a phone.

NewWindowsLauncher

GameShelf for Windows is out

All your games on one shelf, with big box art. Your Steam and Epic games show up by themselves, and when you add a folder of game files GameShelf works out which console each one is for and sets up an emulator to play it. No games are included; bring your own.

Download for Windows

Version 0.1.2, 64-bit Windows, 158 MB zip. Nothing to install: unzip it anywhere and run GameShelf.exe.
